## SecLists

`server/data/passwords/common.txt` and its copy `client/src/lib/commonPasswords.ts` are generated by
`server/scripts/import-common-passwords.ts` from the NCSC "100k most used passwords" list as published in
[SecLists](https://github.com/danielmiessler/SecLists) (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2018 Daniel Miessler). Only
entries of 12 characters or more are kept, in lower case. Taken from commit `1a89b46c204032247592a1626e29f841cb0fe2a5`.

## Bambu Lab error texts (via ha-bambulab)

`server/data/hms/bambu.json` holds what Bambu Lab printers' error codes mean and the address of the page that
explains each. It is generated by `server/scripts/import-hms.ts` from the collected files in
[ha-bambulab](https://github.com/greghesp/ha-bambulab) (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2023 ha-bambulab contributors),
which gathers them from Bambu Lab's own error-text service and wiki. The wording of the messages is Bambu Lab's.
The commit it was taken from is recorded in the file's `source.commit`.
